What is Carrier Screening?

Carrier screening is a type of genetic testing that checks whether a person carries a genetic variant associated with a particular inherited condition.

👥

Why Assess Two Individuals?

Reviewing both individuals' test results can help identify situations that may require further genetic counselling or clinical evaluation.

Understanding the Genetic Test Status

Carrier

The individual has a genetic variant associated with the selected inherited condition but may not show symptoms of that condition.

Not a Carrier

The test did not identify the relevant carrier status being assessed. The meaning of a negative result depends on the test and its limitations.

Affected

The individual has been identified as having the genetic condition. Clinical confirmation and professional interpretation are important.

Understanding the Assessment

Important information about interpreting the details entered in this form.

ℹ️

Why are both test results important?

The genetic status of both individuals may be relevant when evaluating certain inherited conditions. The interpretation depends on the specific condition, the inheritance pattern, and the exact genetic variants involved.

🔬

Importance of confirmed test results

Results based only on assumptions or family history should not be treated as confirmed genetic test results. A qualified healthcare professional or genetic counsellor can help interpret laboratory reports.
